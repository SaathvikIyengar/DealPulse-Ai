> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why DealPulse AI Exists:** Middle-market Private Equity deal teams spend dozens of hours manually building LBO debt schedules in spreadsheets and reviewing data-room disclosures for hidden commercial or financial risks. **DealPulse AI** unites a quantitative Python LBO engine with an AI/NLP due diligence scanner to underwrite buyout targets, stress-test exit multiples, and generate a risk-adjusted **Investment Committee (IC) Memo** in seconds.
